1. Mince ginger/garlic, slice scallion whites; save greens for garnish.
  2. Cut tofu into cubes. Simmer in salted water 2–3 min to firm, set aside.
  3. Heat oil in deep pan. Cook ground meat 6–7 min until browned.
  4. Add scallion whites, ginger, garlic, doubanjiang, chili flakes, peppercorn powder. Sauté 3–4 min.
  5. Deglaze with shaoxing wine 2–3 min. Season with soy sauce, sugar, white pepper.
  6. Add stock and tofu. Bring to a simmer.
  7. Add cornstarch slurry and gently mix until thickened.
  8. Finish with sesame oil. Serve with rice and garnish with scallion greens.
