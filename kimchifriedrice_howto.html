1. In a non-stick pan, cook bacon/pork until slightly browned.
  2. Add minced garlic into leftover oil (add butter if needed).
  3. Add onion and stir until slightly translucent.
  4. Add cooked rice, kimchi + sauce, add gochujang, then taste.
  5. Add sesame oil (about 1 tsp).
  6. Add spring onion.
  7. Add-on (runny eggs): boil egg 6 min, then ice bath to peel easily.
