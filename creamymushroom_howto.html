1. Cook pasta in salted water. Before draining, reserve 3 scoops of pasta water.
  2. Boil chicken stock.
  3. Add butter to pan on high heat.
  4. Add mushrooms and cook. When water comes out, add a pinch of salt and pepper.
  5. When mushrooms are golden, add garlic and cook until golden.
  6. Add wine carefully (optional).
  7. Add stock, cream, parmesan, salt and pepper. Stir and simmer.
  8. Toss pasta into sauce until it coats. If too thick, add reserved pasta water.
