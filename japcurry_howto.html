1. Peel potatoes and chop them into bite-sized pieces; soak in water.
  2. Chop carrots.
  3. Slice onions.
  4. Mince garlic.
  5. Salt beef cubes.
  6. Sear beef with oil.
  7. Caramelize onions with butter till translucent. Add minced garlic.
  8. Add carrots and potatoes.
  9. Add beef stock/chicken stock with water and boil. Cover about 3/4 of ingredients.
  10. After ingredients are cooked, add curry roux. Stir frequently until thickened.
  11. Serve curry with rice.
